😱 The upset rule
Upset = in the NCAA tournament, your team beat a better seed (a bigger seed number beats a smaller one, e.g. a 12 over a 5). Equal seeds never count. Vegas lines are shown for fun but don't decide tournament upsets. Applies to: NCAA tournament.
No eligible games yet — upsets are scored from the NCAA tournament in March. Place your bets responsibly (we mean the figurative kind).

House interpretations
  • Upset bonus applies to NCAA tournament games only (the sheet says "beats a higher seed in the NCAA tournament"). Equal seeds = no upset. First Four upsets: off.
  • A First Four win = +1 play-in and +5 for making the field, but not the +2 NCAA win. A First Four loser: no -1 penalty (just no +5).
  • Games vs non-Division I opponents that ESPN lists count as wins (rule says "any opponent"): yes.
  • NIT/CBI/Crown and other postseason wins don't count. Postponed/canceled games give nothing.
